GM Week 2026 Results

Published 4/9/2026 | Tunnel Vision Weekly #17

By I

The Debate

The GM debate on Wednesday was great. Most seats were taken, but still the vast majority of voters were definitely not there. I feel it was actually pretty useful in getting an informed vote ahead of the big day.

How would you be able to vote for a candidate if you didn't know their middle school binder color choices? The right answer was obviously black for Math, blue for English, green for Science, and red for Social Studies.

Other notable things were the UG President candidates collaborating instead of debating, the Jeopardy theme song ad libbed by some audience members, talking about zodiac signs, and picking favorites on the E-Board in front of everyone.

The Winners

Who won? I'll tell you who won. We won. Everyone won in the end. We all won because we got Wednesday off (and had events and stuff). But also free root beer. I love root beer.

The winners of the elections were announced at the dinner on Friday. Unfortunately I was not there to see, but the results are in anyway!

In case you haven't heard, here are the results of the 2026 GM Week elections!

  • GM: Jordan Krishnayah
  • President of the Union: Kate Goldstein
  • Undergraduate President: Grace Meehan

What a GM Week, fun as always! Hope you guys enjoyed it too!
