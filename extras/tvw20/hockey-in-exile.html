Hockey-in-Exile

Published 10/4/26 | Tunnel Vision Weekly #20

By JSL

I 'm a big sports guy and was really looking forward to the RPI women's hockey game last Friday night—but then a few days before the game, it got moved! Luckily the season hasn't been put on-ice, just on someone else's ice.

If you weren't aware, the Houston Field House was gutted for renovations this summer, and just like me, the school struggles to meet its deadlines and needed an extra few days to put in the new ice.

The team instead played at the M&T Bank Center in Schenectady, and despite the change in venue, we beat Northeastern 2–1 in overtime for our first win of the season! I'm sad I missed it.

That said, I've heard from the administration that our beloved home arena will be ready to go for the men's game this coming Friday, so I hope to see everyone there!
