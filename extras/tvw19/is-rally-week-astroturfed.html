Is Rally Week Astroturfed?

Published 9/13/26 | Tunnel Vision Weekly #19

By JSL

Wait a minute, is the government planning something? Is the government hiding something?

Not our student government, to be clear. They're pretty upfront with their PR, making themselves known when it comes to events they plan.

So when Rally Week started gaining attention without a peep from StuGov, I started to wonder where it really came from. We asked around and learned from several members of StuGov that the entirety of Rally Week is being planned by, like, two or three people.

Who are they? Where are they? None of their posters contain contact info.

For an event seemingly planned to raise school spirit, there isn't a lot that screams “RPI” about it. Not a drop of red on their website!

Naturally I'm led to believe this is a psyop. Some outside firm was hired to inject school spirit within us without realizing what kind of school this is.

Rest assured that they won't get us easily! Possibly due to accounts on Fizz claiming that clubs are pulling out of the event. In any case, we'll be sure to investigate this conspiracy by next issue!
