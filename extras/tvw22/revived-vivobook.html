Revived VivoBook

Published 11/8/26 | Tunnel Vision Weekly #22

By JSL

Last year I found a tiny (like, 10.1”) Asus VivoBook in the Cogswell tech dump.

Last week I bought a new battery for it, installed Linux Mint, and voilà! It's actually functional.

If you're as behind as I am on Linux, this could be how you save a tech-dumped computer yourself! There's only so much to do with Windows 8, after all. Looking forward to doing more with this machine now.
