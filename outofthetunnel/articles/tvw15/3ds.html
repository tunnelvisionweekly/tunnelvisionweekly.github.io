    Anyone Got a 3DS?

    Published 2/25/26 | Out of the Tunnel | Tunnel Vision Weekly #15

    By I

    For this issue's Out of the Tunnel, I is taking over for West Virginia. They'll be back in TVW #16, just a busy schedule.

    Heads up! The 15th Anniversary of the original Nintendo 3DS is in 2 days, 2/26 (JP release date, USA ~1 month later on 3/27)!

    Have you ever used a Nintendo 3DS? For me, it's been a cherished childhood device since I got mine in 2012. Mine's been through a lot, needing a top screen replacement at some point (it was difficult; don't drop it!). It's also been homebrewed! If you haven't homebrewed your 3DS yet, this is your reminder to do so, it's super easy and lets you do so much more with your system, especially since the eShop closed in 2023 (RIP). You can even still use Miiverse and online multiplayer (for certain games) again through the use of Pretendo! For those of you interested, head on over to 3ds.hacks.guide online (for Prentendo, it's pretendo.network)!

    Anyway, this got me wondering, how many people still have 3DSes, especially on campus? Over the past few weeks, I've been feeling nostalgic and been carrying my 3DS everywhere on campus in the hopes of getting a StreetPass (and playing it, of course). I'm sure you'll be shocked, flabbergasted even, to hear that I got a resounding zero StreetPasses so far. I'm stuck on the second-to-last section in Find Mii, I need more StreetPasses! Plus, it would be great to use this feature again!

    If you've got a 3DS, consider carrying it with you to class or around campus (not powered off)! It'd be super cool to get some use out of this unique feature of the 3DS again.
