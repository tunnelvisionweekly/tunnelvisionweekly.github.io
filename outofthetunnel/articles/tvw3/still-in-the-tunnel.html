    But This Week, Still in the Tunnel!

    Published 4/7/25 | Out of the Tunnel | Tunnel Vision Weekly #3

    By I

    Furries. According to popular belief, furries lurk among us, hiding around campus, putting up stickers, et cetera. According to my LA last year, the J-Rowl–JEC tunnel used to be called the "furry tunnel." Not sure how valid that is, lol, but it is what they said on a tour of campus. Lately, there hasn't been a whole lot of that kind of art around the tunnels at all.

    They're definitely still around, though. Only an hour or two after the first ever distribution of Issue #1, I got an email. It was from the RPI Furs, the furry club on campus. Pebbles, a member there, wrote, "The animal people of RPI have a very long tradition of decorating the tunnels (and other places...) with art and stickers," and then offered to send some pictures my way.

    It's actually crazy to me how fast they found a copy. In the spirit of journalism and archival, I accepted. Below you'll find select snippets of art of tunnels long gone. Thank you again for the submission! If you have any art you wanna share from the tunnels, contact us at tunnelvisionweekly@proton.me!
