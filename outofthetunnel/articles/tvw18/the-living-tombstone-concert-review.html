The Living Tombstone

Published 4/21/26 | Out of the Tunnel | Tunnel Vision Weekly #18

By West Virginia

We're taking a break from our regularly scheduled water fountain programming for a very special review of this year's spring concert. It was amazing. Truly. From start to finish it was one of the best concerts I've been to in awhile. The opener was a duo called Boy Jr. and the main performance was a band called The Living Tombstone.

The electro pop duo Boy Jr. had amazing energy and they're bound to blow up soon. They played a few of their newer songs like "Anxious Attachment Avoidant Attachment," which had a fun call-and-response that had everyone in the audience screaming at the top of their lungs. They also played a brand new song just released last month, "I LOVE OTHER PEOPLE EVEN IF I DON'T KNOW THEM." Definitely worth checking them out on Spotify (@Boy Jr.) or anywhere else where you can stream music.

Okay so if I'm being completely honest I only really started listening to The Living Tombstone when I found out they were coming to RPI for this year's spring concert. That being said, I had heard them before, I knew a few of their most popular songs, "Discord" and "No Mercy" for starters, but that's just because I have a phone with Internet access. In preparation for the concert I listened to whatever Apple Music listed as their most popular songs and then eventually just shuffled their whole discography. I can see how it might not be for everyone but I really enjoyed their whole rock, EDM, pop combination. This was their first ever concert on a college campus, and Rust (the green one) even drew Puckman for us while Zero One (the orange one) took over vocals. The whole band had such a great stage presence. I actually can't imagine how hot it must have been up on that stage in those bulky suits, but thankfully UPAC Concerts were blasting the air conditioning in ECAV.

My personal recommendations for getting into their music is as follows: "My Ordinary Life" (arguably their most famous song), "Step On Up," "Jump Up Super Star," "I Wanna Be A Machine," and "Be Alone." A nice mix of old and new stuff!
