Moo-re Water Fountains

Published 3/24/26 | Out of the Tunnel | Tunnel Vision Weekly #16

By West Virginia

Hi all, I'm back with more water fountain rankings. Today we're talking about Sage Lab. Let's start with Sage Lab, quite a strange building. With classrooms that take up 2 stories, a house on the top floor, and a basement filled with student art, Sage labs is definitely one of the more interesting buildings on campus. In my analysis of the campus water fountains I've been exploring these buildings in much more depth than I ever have before. So as I go through and direct you to the best water fountain, I'm also going to share some fun information about these buildings.

Starting from the main floor, which is technically the third floor, entering from the main entrance and heading to the left, you'll find our first water fountain. Although it's not the coldest or most out-of-the-way fountain, it has the best water bottle compatibility and is conveniently located on the main floor of Sage. It's the only fountain in the building with a filtered water bottle dispenser attachment.

Now as we continue through this building you'll find that all the fountains are located in the same spot near the elevator bank on each floor. Much to the inconvenience of anyone in classrooms in the further back half of Sage, those “annexed” classrooms, but I digress.

While still on the first floor, if you instead head all the way to the right, to the classroom at the end of the hall and keep to the leftmost wall of said classroom you'll find you're able to slip out of the windows and get onto the 2nd floor roof. It's also accessible from the boys bathroom on the 3rd floor.

Heading downstairs to the second floor, you'll find a refreshingly cold fountain, but limited water pressure makes it hard to fill certain bottles up. The first floor will not have any water for you, but is home to a wide variety of sculptures and other art pieces made by RPI students.

Let's jump up to the fourth and fifth floors. Both are an older style of fountain, equally annoying to use when trying to fill up a bottle, but the fourth floor does boast a bit more water pressure. Now if you are on the fifth floor and you manage to find the graduate student lounge, located in the “annexed” section, go to the end of the short hallway and on your left there'll be a kitchen/lounge room. If you're able to enter that room there's a door on your immediate left that leads to a classroom*. In that classroom you'll find a ladder that leads up to an attic that is home to some random artifacts, including but not limited to a whole bunch of rocks and a box marked bones (cow?), with what I could only assume were in fact the jaw bones of a cow. Sage is a wonderfully weird building and if I died in there and my soul was forever confined to its walls I would accept that fate graciously.

Overall I'd recommend the second floor fountain. Generally speaking it's gonna be the easiest to access, especially if your class is located in those “annexed” classrooms and for me personally temperature is the most important factor and this water is cold and crisp. Till next time… stay thirsty!

*There is another entrance to this classroom, but it's key card access only and is typically locked, but it's right next to the hallway entrance to the graduate student lounge.
