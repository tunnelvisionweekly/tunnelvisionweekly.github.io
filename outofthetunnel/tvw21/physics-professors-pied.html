Physics Professors Pied

Published 10/20/26 | Out of the Tunnel | Tunnel Vision Weekly #21

By I

If you happened to find yourself in front of Sage Dining Hall around 4 PM on October 6th, you would have been treated to a bizarre sight.

A crowd of students blocking the entire sidewalk, watching as three physics professors—Dr. Ciolek, Dr. Martin, and Dr. Georg—got pied in the face by students and faculty alike.

Pies were $5 each, and it was all a fundraiser for the Physics department, according to the person with the megaphone. People had to stand behind a tape line to toss their pies. It was energetic, people were filming, talking, and there was a general bustle as is typical in a crowd.

Some things of note were that a presumed faculty member spent a whole $20 in a row, also 2-handing the pie trays to slam 2 professors at once. According to The Poly, one student even spent $100 all on Dr. Georg! Also, many people missed their shots, I'm sure if you look in the bushes behind the fence, you'll find lots of free whipped cream!
