Are Halal Shack, Bird N' Brine, and Jimmy John's on the way out?

Published 11/8/26 | Out of the Tunnel | Tunnel Vision Weekly #22

By I

On November 1st, a post on the r/rpi subreddit urged users to sign a petition to stop the closure of Halal Shack, Bird N' Brine, and Jimmy John's from the Union to replace it with a local restaurant, Pita & Olive. This appears to have been first made public by Halal Shack, shortly before the change.org petition was advertised on the subreddit.

There was enough of a chatter around this that the Grand Marshal sent out a mass email asking for opinions on November 3rd. A Google Form is available in this email to submit your opinion, so if you feel so inclined, be sure to check your email!

Meanwhile, on the subreddit, the reactions have been mixed.

Some were against this change, citing the fact that there would be less options to choose from, as well as just the fact that the main dining area on the 1st floor would now only have 1 open location to choose from (plus Wild Blue).

Others, supporting this, added that Pita & Olive are not in fact affiliated or managed by Sodexo, they're a local restaurant to Upstate NY. Additionally, most of the food options would remain, as Pita & Olive reportedly have most of the same food options. This includes Halal, vegetarian, and vegan options. While this change would also increase prices for some items slightly, however, the food would, again, not be managed by Sodexo.

Reportedly, the marketing manager at Halal Shack also responded to this post, saying "Leaving RPI wasn't our choice, and we genuinely wish we could stay. We're grateful for the past seven years and everyone who supported us along the way." While the account cannot be confirmed, a similar statement was made on a banner in the Union.

Additionally, a member of the Student Senate (also, reportedly) outlined many details and concerns that were talked about in a recent Student Senate meeting which went into more detail about this proposal.

There's numerous sides to this debate, and it seems it's just gotten started. If you want to be an official part of it, be sure to check the email from the GM, or, according to the member of the Student Senate, "Reslife and Dining (student advocacy) meets Wednesdays at 6 in the SGS (RU 3120) and there has been a channel created in the stugov discord." We'll keep you updated as we find out more information on this!
