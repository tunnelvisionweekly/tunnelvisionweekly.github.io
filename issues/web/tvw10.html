10 issues, wowie!

—by I

Hooray! We made it to the 10th issue! Truly an exciting milestone for us here! Now we can set our sights for the 100th issue, in... let me do some math... just over 6 years... yeah, maybe that one's a bit too far down the road for now. Still exciting, though!

We've got some goodies in store this time to commemorate the 10th. The first thing you may have noticed was the old logo, a throwback to the first issue, which, granted, only 5 of you will get the reference (though you can see the archives online), I figured it'd be cool to pay homage to it anyway.

In terms of actual special features we're testing the waters with stickers, plus we made a browser-based game to commemorate the tech dumps!

Thank you all again for taking and reading our issues, we wouldn't be here right now if it wasn't for this, so thank you all so much for sticking with us all this time.

Hope you enjoy this fortnight's Tunnel Vision!
