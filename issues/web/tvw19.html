What's New in the Box(es): 2nd Summer Edition

—by I

Hope you're all as excited to get back to tech dumping as I am! Let's start with a recap. According to my sources, not much was seen over the past summer, save for a bunch of "Tek[tronix] scopes and test equipment in Low for Campus Cleanout Day," which sounds exciting, until you hear there were "EHS tech dump police" all around. It happens. On the upside, since the semester started, things have been looking solid! The JEC has seen lots of activity, while the others, not so much.

The find of the week this week has to go to a classic 1977 CRT monitor, which looks straight out of a security office set from a movie. It's big, black-and-white, and the perfect way to break your back!

Coming up in second is a 12-platter HDD in the VCC (now in Low), it weighs a solid 5 lbs, is very shiny, and a fun little trinket for your heavy-duty shelves!

Tunnel Vision: Out of the Tunnel

—by I

Stepping in for West Virginia this issue, I've decided to cover a very sudden and shocking turn of events as of ~9/3.

I was there, minding my own business, walking to the JEC tech dump, when what do I see but a photo of President Marty Schmidt, hanging out on the tech dump box! In my shock, I bolted out, down into the JEC–DCC tunnel.

Boom! Another Marty, right next to the new tunnel paper. In my confusion, I stumble past, only to see the glass doors into the DCC have yet another Marty! Am I hallucinating, I wonder, as at nearly every turn I see another Marty, smiling and enthusiastically greeting me. What does he want from me?? I retreated to my safehouse to deliberate what this could all mean.

Here's what we know so far: he's been spotted at the JEC tech dump, DCC tech dump, a DCC stairwell, the JEC staircase, by WRPI (twice), and I'm sure there's more places that we are yet to discover! Who (or what?) is putting up these signs? Has Marty become omnipotent via the help of alumni funding? We sure don't have the answers, but it sure has been a wild ride seeing him everywhere. If you see another Marty poster, or know who is placing them, let us know! The mystery must be solved, and we can't do it without you!

On Borrowed Time

After & Before Graduation

—by JSL

As mentioned amidst the random news of this issue, we're short-staffed after several of our members have graduated, and I'm actually one of them.

So why am I still at RPI? No way I'd go for a co-term. Throughout the four years I've spent here, I've failed a record six classes, not to mention one I had to late-drop in order to prevent that tally from hitting seven.

This is a reality some people here are going to face, but you can actually walk at graduation even if you're a few credits short! That's what I did.

What I do with my time now is a bigger question. I never thought I'd be here for even longer, but it does seem befitting for us as an underground publication to cover what it's like to essentially “haunt” RPI.

It might also help to know if you find yourself in my position that it's not the end of the world. Stay posted for more of this section on this extra semester!

Extra: Is Rally Week Astroturfed?

—by JSL

Wait a minute, is the government planning something? Is the government hiding something?

Not our student government, to be clear. They're pretty upfront with their PR, making themselves known when it comes to events they plan.

So when Rally Week started gaining attention without a peep from StuGov, I started to wonder where it really came from. We asked around and learned from several members of StuGov that the entirety of Rally Week is being planned by, like, two or three people.

Who are they? Where are they? None of their posters contain contact info.

For an event seemingly planned to raise school spirit, there isn't a lot that screams “RPI” about it. Not a drop of red on their website!

Naturally I'm led to believe this is a psyop. Some outside firm was hired to inject school spirit within us without realizing what kind of school this is.

Rest assured that they won't get us easily! Possibly due to accounts on Fizz claiming that clubs are pulling out of the event. In any case, we'll be sure to investigate this conspiracy by next issue!
