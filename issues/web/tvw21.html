Tunnel Vision: Out of the Tunnel

Physics Professors Pied

—By I

If you happened to find yourself in front of Sage Dining Hall around 4 PM on October 6th, you would have been treated to a bizarre sight.

A crowd of students blocking the entire sidewalk, watching as three physics professors—Dr. Ciolek, Dr. Martin, and Dr. Georg—got pied in the face by students and faculty alike.

Pies were $5 each, and it was all a fundraiser for the Physics department, according to the person with the megaphone. People had to stand behind a tape line to toss their pies. It was energetic, people were filming, talking, and there was a general bustle as is typical in a crowd.

Some things of note were that a presumed faculty member spent a whole $20 in a row, also 2-handing the pie trays to slam 2 professors at once. According to The Poly, one student even spent $100 all on Dr. Georg! Also, many people missed their shots, I'm sure if you look in the bushes behind the fence, you'll find lots of free whipped cream!

Tech Dumps: A PSA

—by I

It's come to our attention that some people have been selling computer and similar parts for scraps from the tech dumps.

While this may seem like an easy way to get money, and I know, college is expensive, the tech dumps here at RPI are a sacred place.

They allow us students to peruse the various technology that was already going to be thrown out and sold for scraps by RPI, and save it from this fate. Sure, many items may be old, but there's still plenty of uses for old PCs even if you yourself don't see them. It could be, for example, for playing retro PC games or running old software, getting obsolete out-of-manufacture PC parts to repair machines, stuff like that.

If you or someone you know is scrapping these fully working electronics, or, say, destroying items to get to the traces, you're (or they are) effectively degrading what the tech dumps have been for years. It seems like an easy way to get money but again, taking stuff from the tech dumps with the intention to sell as-is is just underhanding everyone else at the tech dumps.

If anything, we ask that you be considerate for others who may be using the tech dumps, competition doesn't make the tech dumps any better, it just degrades the experience for everyone.

Let's preserve the tech dumps as the place where you take what you actually are going to use, and not as a place you can get a quick buck over other tech dump users.

Thank you.

Borrowed Time

Half a Season to Remember

—by JSL

I'm on a three-win streak! As far as attending hockey games goes, I've been to all the men's games we've won at home so far, and it's been a blast.

There's something more to this year, though, and not just with the new ice. Not just with the new team benches, the new glass, the new doors, or the new snow pits they put in so the zambonis don't have to dump ice outside anymore.

There's a new feeling I get when I get to the field house, like I'm watching the same hockey team I first saw in action my freshman year. These guys look stronger than ever.

Last Friday was also the very first themed hockey night! The Blackout game and the Big Red Freakout used to be it, but they're trying something brand new, and I'm all here for it.

Especially because this first one was Star Wars night. They gave out free lightsabers (though I also brought my own) and announced our starting lineup to Duel of the Fates. Awesome stuff, and I'm sad I won't be here for next year's.
