Half a Season to Remember

Published 10/20/26 | Borrowed Time | Tunnel Vision Weekly #21

By JSL

I 'm on a three-win streak! As far as attending hockey games goes, I've been to all the men's games we've won at home so far, and it's been a blast.

There's something more to this year, though, and not just with the new ice. Not just with the new team benches, the new glass, the new doors, or the new snow pits they put in so the zambonis don't have to dump ice outside anymore.

There's a new feeling I get when I get to the field house, like I'm watching the same hockey team I first saw in action my freshman year. These guys look stronger than ever.

Last Friday was also the very first themed hockey night! The Blackout game and the Big Red Freakout used to be it, but they're trying something brand new, and I'm all here for it.

Especially because this first one was Star Wars night. They gave out free lightsabers (though I also brought my own) and announced our starting lineup to Duel of the Fates. Awesome stuff, and I'm sad I won't be here for next year's.
