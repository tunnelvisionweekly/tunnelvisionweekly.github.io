The Best Years for Parties

Published 11/8/26 | Borrowed Time | Tunnel Vision Weekly #22

By JSL

How was your Halloween? Seriously, if you're on our Discord server, I'd love to know how our average reader spends the holiday. Mine was awesome, and unlike anything I ever expected.

Firstly, the pre-party we went to was at my old apartment from two years ago! I recognized it as soon as my friends sent me the address. Absolutely bizarre and appropriately haunting to see my old room almost exactly as I left it. I felt like a real ghost.

Following that, though, I felt as alive as ever on the party scene. Not sure if I should say where I went in case PubSafe happens to read our ever-so-mainstream paper, but I will proudly say that I'm friends with the wizards.

And friends are both what'll get you into parties and what you should get out of them. There's no big secret to it. Treat parties like networking events! It's a great way to make connections and have a good time with new people, so even if you're not the biggest party person, step out of the tunnel and try it sometime!

If this is the last party I have time for in my last semester, I'll take it.
