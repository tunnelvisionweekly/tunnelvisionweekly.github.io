Before & After Graduation

Published 9/13/26 | On Borrowed Time | Tunnel Vision Weekly #19

By JSL

As mentioned amidst the random news of this issue, we're short-staffed after several of our members have graduated, and I'm actually one of them.

So why am I still at RPI? No way I'd go for a co-term. Throughout the four years I've spent here, I've failed a record six classes, not to mention one I had to late-drop in order to prevent that tally from hitting seven.

This is a reality some people here are going to face, but you can actually walk at graduation even if you're a few credits short! That's what I did.

What I do with my time now is a bigger question. I never thought I'd be here for even longer, but it does seem befitting for us as an underground publication to cover what it's like to essentially “haunt” RPI.

It might also help to know if you find yourself in my position that it's not the end of the world. Stay posted for more of this section on this extra semester!
